About these numbers

Tax brackets, basic personal amounts and payroll rates are the 2026 figures published by the Canada Revenue Agency and Revenu Québec. Bracket includes the basic personal amount, Canada employment amount, CPP or QPP and EI credits, and Ontario's surtax, health premium and low-income reduction. It leaves out other credits and deductions, so treat results as estimates, not tax advice.

Income ranks by age use the 2021 Census (Statistics Canada table 98-10-0064-01, total income in 2020), adjusted to today's dollars with the CRA's yearly inflation indexing. Job pay comes from Job Bank median wages. Course times and costs come from the schools, regulators and guides linked in Career, Level up. Retirement defaults follow FP Canada's 2026 planning assumptions, and CPP and OAS amounts are Service Canada's 2026 figures. Rent-or-buy uses 2026 mortgage rules (minimum down payment, default-insurance premiums, the OSFI stress test) and Ontario and BC land transfer tax rules; other assumptions are editable estimates. Cost of living compares Statistics Canada's Market Basket Measure (2018 base): what a modest, basic standard of living costs in each of the 15 biggest cities, rent included.
